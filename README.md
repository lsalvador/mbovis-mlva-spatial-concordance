#Mycobacterium bovis MLVA spatial analysis in cattle and badgers# 
This repository contains R code developed for the study "Scale- and genotype-dependent spatial concordance of Mycobacterium bovis in cattle and badgers" authored by Assel Akhmetova, Liliana C. M. Salvador*, Katarina Oravcova, Eleanor Presho, Carl McCormick, Suzan Thompson, Lorraine Wright, Fraser Menzies, Nigel Trimble, Roland Harwood, Rowland R. Kao, Adrian Allen, Theo Pepler+, Robin Skuce+ (* corresponding author; + equal contribution). This study was funded by JSC “Center for International Programs” “Bolashak” Scholarship, DAERA Evidence and Innovation Projects 15/3/07 and 18/3/12 and UK’s Biotechnology and Biological Sciences Research Council (BBSRC) – grant numbers BB/P0105598 and BB/M01262X.
